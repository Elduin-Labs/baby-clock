# Baby Clock

A clock that turns you into a baby! 👶

Place the Baby Clock and it starts ticking: tick, tock, and with every tick you
get a little smaller until you're a tiny baby with your mouth hanging open,
drooling all over yourself. Right-click the clock to tick back up into a
grown-up. You'll find it in the **Baby** tab in Creative, or craft it from a
clock and a milk bucket.

- **GitHub:** the mod files are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/Elduin-Labs/baby-clock/releases)
